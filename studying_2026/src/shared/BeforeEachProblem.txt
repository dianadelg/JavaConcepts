Before doing any problem, ask yourself:
1. What is the input? Array, string, graph, tree, linked list?
2. What is the question asking? Existence, shortest path, count, maximum, minimum?
3. Are there special clues? Sorted input, contiguous elements, connected nodes, repeated subproblems?
4. Which algorithms could solve it? Sometimes there are multiple correct answers.
5. Why would I choose one over another? Complexity, implementation simplicity, memory, or constraints?
